Founding Fellows Program

Join a student AI lab that actually ships.

Fellows work in small teams on AI research, software products, and community-impact tools.

Who should apply?

We are looking for high school and early college students who want to contribute seriously to AI projects. You do not need a perfect resume, but you should be willing to learn fast, communicate clearly, and finish what you start.

Tracks

AI Research

Experiments, literature reviews, evaluation frameworks, benchmarks, and responsible AI analysis.

Software Engineering

Frontend, backend, APIs, data pipelines, AI integrations, and open-source project development.

Product and Design

User research, UI/UX, product strategy, documentation, testing, and launch support.

Expectations

  • Commit 3–5 hours per week.
  • Contribute to at least one project.
  • Attend periodic check-ins or async updates.
  • Produce public work: demo, repository, report, or presentation.

Application

To apply, email your answers to the questions below. Include any project links, GitHub repositories, portfolios, or research work that show what you can contribute.

  1. Name, grade, school, city/state
  2. Email and optional LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio
  3. Which track are you applying for?
  4. What have you built, researched, designed, or led?
  5. Why do you want to join Stratum AI Labs?
  6. What AI project would you want to work on?
  7. What skills can you contribute immediately?
  8. Can you commit 3–5 hours per week?
  9. Are you comfortable with this being purely an experience and knowledge role? (Unpaid)
